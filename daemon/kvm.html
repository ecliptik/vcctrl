Input
Toggles Caps Lock and waits for the PS/2 LED to come back. Worth running when the target stops responding and you need to know which half is at fault.
Mouse speed
The mouse is relative-only, the same as a real PS/2 or ADB mouse: there is no "put the cursor here", only "move it". Park re-homes it to a known corner when it has drifted from what the picture shows.
